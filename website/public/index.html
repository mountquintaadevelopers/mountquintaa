One Plot, Three Ways to Enjoy It

Your Weekend Villa

A cosy 1 BHK or a roomy family villa, your choice and your budget. Leave the city on Friday evening, and by Saturday morning the only plan is breakfast on the verandah.

Your Own Garden

The green 60% of your plot is yours to shape. Plant a mango sapling with your kids, grow your own tomatoes, or just hang a hammock and do nothing at all.
