Our Story

Built for Families Who Want More Than a Plot

Mount Quintaa started with a simple wish: a place where families can slow down together, not just a piece of land on paper.

Who We Are

The People Behind Mount Quintaa

Mount Quintaa is developed by Mohit and Ravi, together with Ravi's two sons, who look after construction and land aggregation. We're a family too, and we're building the kind of place we'd want for our own.

We're a small, hands-on team, and that's on purpose. The people you meet on your first visit are the same people building the roads, the clubhouse, and the community around your plot.

There's no middle layer. Our marketing partner only helps with sales. Once you're an owner, you talk to us directly about the project, construction, documents, anything at all.
