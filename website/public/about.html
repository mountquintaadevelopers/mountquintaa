Our Story

Rooted in Nature, Built for Generations

Mount Quintaa began with one belief: owning farmland shouldn't demand a farmer's life.

Who We Are

Land people, not landing pages

We're a Bangalore-based team of agronomists, land-title experts and estate managers working on the Hosur–Shoolagiri farm belt of Krishnagiri district, Tamil Nadu. Before a single plot is sold, we verify decades of ownership history, convert and fence the land, lay irrigation, and plant the first trees. What you buy is not a promise — it's a working farm with your name on the deed.

Today, over a hundred families across Bangalore and abroad own their piece of Mount Quintaa. Most found us the same way you did — searching for farm plots near Bangalore they could actually trust.

What We Stand For

Three promises we never break

Transparency First

Every document — mother deed, EC, survey sketch — is shared before you commit. Our lawyers will walk you through all of it, and we encourage you to bring your own.

Farms That Actually Farm

Managed farmland only works if someone manages it. Our resident team lives on-site, and every owner receives monthly photo and growth updates of their plot.

Community Over Sales

We host harvest festivals, farm workshops and family weekends. We'd rather grow a neighbourhood slowly than sell plots quickly.
